I study how large language models, including multimodal ones, can extract structured information from long, complex documents such as corporate sustainability reports and planning documents, and how we can tell whether the extracted data is right.

Before the PhD I studied Computational Linguistics (M.Sc., LMU Munich), Mathematics (M.Sc., RWTH Aachen) and Economics (B.Sc., University of Mannheim), and worked as a software and NLP engineer, including on the open-source education platform Serlo.
